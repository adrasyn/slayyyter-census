The maximum penalty for failing to complete the Census when you have been directed to is $364 per day. This page multiplies that figure by the time elapsed, and does not stop.

This is a joke and it is not a bill. Nobody is fined merely for being late. The $364 daily penalty applies only after the Australian Bureau of Statistics serves a written Notice of Direction, the direction is still refused, and a court convicts — see the ABS on what actually happens.
